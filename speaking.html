Short bioFor quick introductions

Ayoola Timilehin Israel is a final year Mechatronics Engineering student and intelligent systems builder working across embedded systems, IoT, automation and sustainable engineering. He also leads technical communities and learning programmes that have expanded access to AI, engineering and career opportunities for young people.

Long bioFor conferences and panels

Ayoola Timilehin Israel is a final year B.Tech Mechatronics Engineering student at the Federal University of Technology, Minna. His work focuses on real world hardware intelligence across embedded systems, IoT infrastructure, autonomous control, AI assisted monitoring and sustainable energy. He has developed projects in smart concrete verification, automated fire response, robotic surveillance and climate focused innovation. Beyond engineering, Ayoola has served as Millennium Fellowship Campus Director, IEEE FUT Minna Programmes Lead, NSBE Founding Secretary and a trainer with Windows on America and Summer With Her. Through workshops, mentorship and community programmes, he has helped learners gain practical access to artificial intelligence, engineering and career development.
