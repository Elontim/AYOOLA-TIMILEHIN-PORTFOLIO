About

Technology should solve real problems—and create room for more people.

My work sits where hardware, intelligence and human need meet.

I study Mechatronics Engineering at the Federal University of Technology, Minna. I prototype sensor systems, train computer-vision models, design automation workflows and explore resilient energy systems.

Beyond engineering, I lead programmes that help students and young women gain practical access to AI, digital skills and career opportunities. That combination—building systems and building people—is the thread through everything I do.
