Engineering profile

Technology should solve real problems and create room for more people.

My work sits where hardware, intelligence and human need meet.

I am a final year B.Tech Mechatronics Engineering student at the Federal University of Technology, Minna, focused on robotics, IoT ecosystems, embedded systems and autonomous control systems.

My engineering work centres on real world hardware intelligence, including sensor based concrete verification, automated fire detection and suppression, and WiFi enabled robotic surveillance. These projects integrate embedded systems, control theory and real time data acquisition.

I currently focus on scalable solutions in embedded systems, IoT infrastructure, control systems modelling and AI assisted monitoring for operational challenges in energy, safety and sustainable development.
