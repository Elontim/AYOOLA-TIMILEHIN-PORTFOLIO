My story

Technology should solve real problems and create room for more people.

My work sits where hardware, intelligence and human need meet.

I study Mechatronics Engineering at the Federal University of Technology, Minna. I prototype sensor systems, design automation workflows, develop intelligent solutions and explore resilient energy systems.

Beyond engineering, I lead programmes that help students and young women gain practical access to AI, digital skills and career opportunities. Building systems and building people remain the central thread through everything I do.
